The Other 9 Evaluated Models & Specific Failure Causes

MODEL 02 MELTED WAX
Trimmed Poisson Mesh Watertight isosurface assumption rounds sharp desk edges and generates artificial convex bubbles across unobserved door openings.
MODEL 03 PINHOLE GAPS
Ball-Pivoting Mesh (BPA) Rolling sphere triangulation respects room hollows, but leaves prominent triangular perforations on low-texture whitewashed walls.
MODEL 04 AISLE BRIDGING
Alpha-Shapes Hull Alpha concave radius bridges across empty corridors between adjacent student desks, obstructing navigable pathways.
MODEL 05 SOFT BEZELS
TSDF Marching Cubes 3cm voxel discretization dampens high-frequency geometry, softening sharp monitor bezels, power outlets, and ceiling fan rods.
MODEL 06 OVER-SIMPLIFIED
Planar CAD BIM Shell Multi-model RANSAC isolates perimeter floor, ceiling, and walls, but completely ignores interior desks, chairs, and whiteboard.
MODEL 07 BLOCKY CUBES
3D Voxel Lattice Generates 5cm³ cubic occupancy voxels. Excellent for robotic collision checks, but aesthetically unsuitable for a human digital twin.
MODEL 08 NON-RGB TENSORS
LingBot-Map GCT Encodes geometric curvature tensors into vertex colors for neural segmentation, replacing natural photographic color.
MODEL 09 NO SURFACES
CAD Bounding Boxes Represents detected objects as colored 3D bounding wireframes; valuable for semantic inventory, but lacks visual realism.
MODEL 10 HIGH DRAW CALLS
Oriented Surfels Disks Renders tangent circular surfels. While superior to triangles, surfels incur 3× higher draw call overhead in WebGL than continuous point buffers.

1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Spatial reconstruction from low-cost consumer smartphones without hardware LiDAR suffers from two critical, interconnected limitations: (1) monocular scale ambiguity, where metric distances cannot be recovered from uncalibrated RGB pixels alone, and (2) temporal drift, where unbounded visual-inertial integration curves straight corridors into bananas.

Mathematical Scale Anchor:
s* = argmin_s ∑ || a_imu - d²(s · t_vio)/dt² - g ||²

By anchoring visual odometry acceleration to STMicro hardware gravity (&vec;g = [0, -9.81, 0]^T), scale factor s is disambiguated with zero LiDAR.

Core Contributions & Architectural Novelty:

  • Hardware-Anchored Monocular VIO: PoseCam native Kotlin Android engine logging STMicro IMU at 462.2 Hz synchronized to image sensor frame exposures.
  • Height-Bandpass Elevation Slicing: Isolates obstacles within [0.08m, 1.80m], eliminating floor rugs and overhead ceiling noise.
  • The 10-Model CAD Suite: Rigorous empirical evaluation across 10 distinct 3D representations, crowning Model 1 (Dense Metric Cloud) as champion.

4. Height-Bandpass Costmap & ROS 2 Navigation

Autonomous mobile robots operating in indoor spaces must differentiate between walkable floor surfaces, traversable carpet cords, overhead lighting fixtures, and lethal collision obstacles.

Elevation Slicing Formulation:
P_obs = { p ∈ P | y_floor + 0.08m ≤ y_p ≤ y_floor + 1.80m }

Points below 8cm (power cables, carpet trims) and above 1.80m (ductwork, lighting) are discarded. Remaining obstacle points are projected to a 5cm 2D occupancy grid.

ROS 2 Nav2 Map Server Parameters:

Resolution: 0.05 m/px · Origin: [-4.25, -3.10, 0.00] · Occupied Threshold: 0.65 · Free Threshold: 0.196. Obstacle dilation radius r = 0.22m prevents chassis scraping.
