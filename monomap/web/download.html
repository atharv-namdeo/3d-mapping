How to Shoot the Perfect Room Scan

1

Install & Allow ARCore

Open the downloaded APK on your Android device. If prompted, allow "Install from Unknown Sources". PoseCam requires Google Play Services for AR (standard on modern Androids).

2

Arm the Tracking Gate (Stand Still 3s)

Open PoseCam. Hold the camera up and stay still for 3 seconds until the top indicator turns green. This locks the phone's hardware accelerometer to true Earth gravity.

3

Walk the Perimeter (Steady Pace)

Tap Record. Walk smoothly around the room perimeter at a calm walking pace. Avoid quick wrist jerks. Loop back so your ending spot overlaps the start by 1–2 metres.

4

Export Session (One Tap)

Tap Stop → tap Sessions → tap your recording → tap Save to Downloads (or plug your phone into PC via USB to copy the captures/ folder).

5

Run Reconstruction on Laptop

Transfer the session folder to your laptop and execute:

python src/run_pipeline.py --posecam path/to/capture-XXXX --name room_scan
